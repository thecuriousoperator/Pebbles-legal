Last updated: 1 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Pebbles LLC collects, uses, shares and retains via the app during the pilot phase for a closed group of invited participants.

Who We Are

Pebbles LLC is granting you access to a relationship intelligence application called “Pebbles” during the specified pilot period in accordance with the Terms of Use.

Personal Data We Collect

We collect via the app the following categories of personal data:

Data you provide directly to Pebbles LLC

  • Account information (name, email address)
  • Relationship notes, conversation logs, and impressions you enter about your contacts
  • Preferences and settings you configure within the app

If you submit any personal data relating to other people to us, you represent that you have the authority to do so and to permit us to use the information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Data collected automatically by the app

  • Basic usage data (which features you use, how often)
  • Technical information (device type, operating system)

Data collected by Apple Inc. via your device

  • Device information, usage information and crash data

We do not

  • Access your phone’s contacts without your consent (e.g. opt in via the app)
  • Collect location data
  • Collect sensitive personal data (please see the restriction on uploading sensitive personal data in the Terms of Use)

How We Use Your Personal Data

We may use your personal data to:

  • Provide and operate the Pebbles app
  • Debug and troubleshoot technical and other issues
  • Respond to your questions or requests
  • Improve product functionality and inform future development of the app

We do not use your personal data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond those stated herein. We also do not sell your data to third parties.

How Your Personal Data Is Shared

The categories of third parties with whom we may share your personal data include:

  • Service providers and contractors we use to provide the service and support the pilot
  • Other recipients when required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities
  • Those persons with respect to which you have provided your consent

Your Rights

Subject to certain exceptions and depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access your personal data as processed by us and, if you believe that any information relating to you is incorrect, obsolete or incomplete, to request its correction or updating;
  • request the erasure of your personal data or the restriction thereof to specific categories of processing;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting prior processing;
  • object, in whole or in part, to the processing of your personal data, including for direct marketing and research (with certain exceptions);
  • request data portability, i.e. to receive or transfer your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format; and
  • object to automated decision-making including profiling if it has a significant or legal impact on you.

You may also have the right to object at any time, in accordance with law, to our processing of your personal data where such processing is based on our or a third party’s legitimate interest.

You have the right to be free from unlawful discrimination for exercising your rights under applicable law.

Some states (including but not limited to California, Texas and Virginia) have specific rights for their residents. For information about those rights, see the Supplemental US Privacy Rights section below.

To exercise your rights under applicable law, contact us by email at anneka@withpebbles.com.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted under applicable law.

Personal Data Protection

Pebbles LLC uses technical and organisational measures designed to guard against unauthorised access or other use of your personal data. While we take reasonable precautions, no system is completely secure.

Children’s Privacy

Pebbles is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware that a minor has provided us with personal data, we will delete it promptly.
