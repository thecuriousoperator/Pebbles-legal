Privacy Policy

Pilot Program

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Pebbles LLC collects, uses, shares, and retains via the app during the pilot phase for a closed group of invited participants.

Who We Are

Pebbles LLC is granting you access to a relationship intelligence application called “Pebbles” during the specified pilot period in accordance with the Terms of Use.

Personal Data We Collect

We collect via the app the following categories of personal data:

Data you provide directly to Pebbles LLC

  • Account information (e.g. name, email address, school, class year)
  • Information you choose to record about your contacts
  • Preferences and settings you configure within the app
  • You may record personal information about your contacts in Pebbles. Please add only information you have a legitimate reason to keep, and that the person would reasonably expect you to know. Do not record sensitive information about others, such as health, religion, sexual orientation, or financial details. If you submit personal data relating to other people, you represent that you have the authority to do so and to permit us to use the information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

If you use the dictation feature, your device’s built-in speech recognition converts your speech to text. We receive only the resulting text.

Data from your iPhone, only with your permission

  • Notifications. If you allow notifications from the app, we store:
    • a device token issued by Apple
    • your time zone
    • your notification settings

Our servers use your Pebbles data to decide which reminders to send (for example, before or after a calendar event, when a follow-up is due, or a weekly preview of how many contacts with which you have scheduled a calendar event). Reminders are delivered through Apple’s Push Notification service. A notification may include a contact’s name, a goal you wrote in the app for the calendar event, a note you wrote in the app the last time you met with a contact, or a calendar event name, and may be visible on your lock screen depending on your device settings. We keep a record of each notification sent, including its text, to avoid sending duplicates and to improve the timing and usefulness of reminders. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.

  • Contacts. If you allow Pebbles to access your iPhone Contacts, the app will search the contacts on your device as you type a name in the app so that the app can suggest a match. The app will only save the information of those contacts you choose to add to the app, and will only include the contact name and, where available, email address(es), phone number(s), company, job title, birthday, partner’s name, kids’ names, city, and LinkedIn profile URL. The rest of your address book is not uploaded or stored by Pebbles.
  • Calendar. If you connect your iPhone Calendar to the app, Pebbles reads calendar events from the past 14 days and the next 14 days on your device to find meetings with people whose information you’ve saved in Pebbles as a contact. In relation to those events, Pebbles only stores:
    • the date and start time of such events, and the name and dates of events Pebbles identifies as conferences
    • an identifier for each calendar event
    • when your iPhone calendar was last read

Event names, notes, locations, and other attendees’ names and email addresses are processed on your device and are not stored by Pebbles, with two exceptions:

  • if you choose to add an event attendee as a new contact in the app, their name, email address and the event title will auto-fill in the new contact form in the app; you can elect to delete any such information from the contact before saving it in the app
  • if you confirm that a calendar event attendee is one of your existing app contacts, the email address listed in the calendar invite will automatically be added into that contact’s record in the app

Data collected automatically by the app

  • Usage data (e.g. which features you use, how often)
  • Technical information (e.g. device type, operating system)
  • Device token for push notifications and your device’s time zone

We do not

  • Access your iPhone Contacts or Calendar without your permission. You can withdraw permission at any time in your iPhone Settings
  • Upload into the app your device’s full address book or calendar
  • Collect location data
  • Collect sensitive personal data other than your account login (please see restriction on uploading to the app sensitive personal data in the Terms of Use)

How We Use Your Personal Data

We may use your personal data to:

  • Provide and operate the app
  • Debug and troubleshoot technical and other issues
  • Send those reminders and notifications you have enabled within the app
  • Match calendar events on your iPhone calendar to the contacts you’ve saved in the app
  • Respond to your questions or requests submitted to Pebbles LLC
  • Improve product functionality and inform future development of the app

We do not use your personal data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond those stated herein. We also do not sell your data to third parties.

How Your Personal Data Is Shared

The categories of third parties with whom we may share your personal data include:

  • Service providers and contractors we use to provide the service and support the pilot, including our hosting provider and Apple’s Push Notification service, which delivers notifications to your device
  • Other recipients when required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities
  • Those persons with respect to which you have provided your consent
